This is the first post on this blog.

## How this site is built

- **Hugo** turns Markdown posts into static web pages.
- **PaperMod** is the theme.
- **GitHub Pages** hosts the site for free.
- **GitHub Actions** builds and publishes the site after every change.

## How to write a new post

Open [Pages CMS](https://app.pagescms.org), sign in with GitHub, choose this repository, then create a post and save it. The site updates by itself a minute or two later.
